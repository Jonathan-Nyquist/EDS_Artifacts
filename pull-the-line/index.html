Pull the Line

Fifteen cricket chirp rates and the temperatures they were recorded at. Some points hold the regression line steady. Some can drag it wherever they go. Write your prediction on the handout before you lock anything in.

Your line

Fit by eye

Drag the two round handles up and down until the line fits the cloud as well as you can. The handles also move with the arrow keys.

Score: the sum of your squared vertical misses, divided by least squares'. A perfect line scores 1.00. Nothing can score lower.

Find the plant

Each dataset follows the same kind of chirp–temperature trend, but someone moved one point 20 °F up or down before the line was fit. The residual plot underneath shows each point's miss from that line.

Before you accuse, you get two probes. A probe removes one point, refits the line without it, and reports how much the slope changed and how far the removed point sits from the new line.
